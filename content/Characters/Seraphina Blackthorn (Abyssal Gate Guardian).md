Abyssal fiend (human-demon hybrid), chaotic neutral

**Seraphina Blackthorn** is a half-fiend warlock and sorcerer with a dark connection to the Abyss. She is the guardian of the Abyssal Gate in Ravenmoor, wielding eldritch power and commanding fiendish creatures. Though she seeks redemption, her fiendish heritage makes her an unpredictable ally or adversary.

### **Appearance**

Seraphina stands at 5'9" with a commanding presence. Her long, platinum blonde hair shimmers faintly as if reflecting the chaotic energy of her demonic heritage. Her piercing violet eyes glow with an inner light, hinting at the eldritch power she wields. Pale, almost porcelain skin is marked with intricate black runes that shimmer when she channels her magic. She wears a sleek black outfit adorned with silver accents, Gothic motifs, and glowing eldritch runes, blending armor with demonic motifs like skull ornaments and crescent moon pendants.

---

### **Traits**

**Fiendish Resilience**  
Seraphina has resistance to fire and necrotic damage.

**Darkvision**  
Seraphina can see in both magical and non-magical darkness up to 120 feet.

**Abyssal Warding**  
As a bonus action, Seraphina can create a ward of dark energy around herself, granting +2 to her AC and resistance to all damage types except radiant for 1 minute.

**Cursed Nature - Abyssal Burden**  
Whenever Seraphina takes radiant damage, she must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or be stunned until the end of her next turn.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**  
Seraphina makes two attacks with **Riftbreaker** or casts **Eldritch Blast** twice.

**Riftbreaker Slash**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 2d10 + 5 slashing damage + 2d6 necrotic damage. The target must make a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.

**Eldritch Blast**  
_Ranged Spell Attack_: +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 3d10 force damage.

**Rift Slash (Recharge 5–6)**  
Seraphina channels the power of **Riftbreaker** to slash open a rift in reality, creating a 30-foot line of necrotic energy. Each creature in the line must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or take 6d6 necrotic damage and be knocked prone.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Seraphina can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. She can only use one legendary action at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn.

1. **Shadowstep**: Seraphina teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space she can see.
    
2. **Eldritch Shield**: Seraphina raises a shield of dark energy, gaining +2 to her AC until the start of her next turn.
    
3. **Fiendish Grasp (Costs 2 Actions)**: Seraphina reaches out with a spectral hand, forcing a creature within 30 feet to make a DC 18 Strength saving throw. On a failure, the creature is restrained and takes 3d6 necrotic damage at the start of its turn for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
    

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Abyssal Command (1/day)**  
Seraphina can summon one fiendish creature (CR 5 or lower) to aid her in combat for 1 hour. The creature obeys her commands and acts on her initiative.

**Fiendish Rebuke (2/day)**  
As a reaction to being hit by an attack, Seraphina can cause the attacker to make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, the attacker takes 4d10 fire damage (half on success).

**Riftbreaker’s Defense**  
Seraphina can create a protective barrier of shadow energy around herself or an ally, granting resistance to all damage for 1 round.

**Riftwalk (3/day)**  
Seraphina can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space she can see, leaving behind a portal that can be used by allies until the start of her next turn

---

### **Backstory**

Born to a human mother and a powerful fiendish entity, Seraphina was marked from birth by dark forces. After being forced into the service of Lord Damaris, she embraced her demonic heritage for survival. Zar'vhal sensed her demonic awakening and bargained her escape. In return for escaping, she is bound to command and guard the **Abyssal Gate** in Ravenmoor, the only known way in and out of the Abyss. Accepting his terms, he bestowed upon her the weapon **Riftbreaker**. Weilding Riftbreaker, she escaped her captors and fled to the **Abyssal Gate**. She has resided there ever since, upholding her bargain as the guardian.
