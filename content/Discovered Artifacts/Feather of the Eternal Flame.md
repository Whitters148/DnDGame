_Wondrous Item, artifact, requires attunement by a creature of pure heart (usually cleric, paladin, or fire-aligned spellcaster)_

---

## **Description**

The **Feather of the Eternal Flame** is a radiant quill that shifts in hue from deep crimson to gold, shimmering with fire that never burns away. Warm to the touch and weightless, it carries the essence of **Ashara Everdawn’s predecessor, the first phoenix**, embodying the eternal cycle of destruction and renewal.

Once entrusted to the **Order of Eternal Flame** as a sacred relic, it has since been stolen by the **Sand Striders**, leaving the desert unbalanced and vulnerable to corruption. Wherever it rests, the air shimmers with heat, and whispers of renewal ripple through the flames.

---

## **Properties**

While attuned to the Feather, the wielder gains:

- **Sacred Flame.** Suppresses curses and Abyssal corruption in a 30-foot radius.
    
- **Eternal Warmth.** Wielder and allies within 10 feet gain **immunity to cold damage** and **advantage on Constitution saves vs exhaustion**.
    
- **Blessing of Renewal.** Wielder and allies within 10 feet require only **half the normal time for long rests**, rejuvenated by phoenix fire.
    
- **Elemental Resistance.** The wielder has **immunity to cold damage** and **resistance to fire damage**.
    

---

## **Magical Abilities**

- **Ignite Weapon (1/Short Rest).** As a bonus action, the wielder ignites a weapon with phoenix fire for 1 minute. All attacks with that weapon deal an additional **2d8 fire damage**.
    
- **Phoenix Rebirth (1/Long Rest).** When reduced to 0 hit points, the Feather erupts in flame. All creatures within 30 feet take **6d10 fire damage** (Dexterity save DC 18 for half). The wielder regains **half their maximum HP** and stabilises.
    
    - **Player Awareness:** Allies can anticipate the rebirth if close, giving them the chance to avoid it.
        
    - **Enemy Awareness:** Creatures with **high Perception or passive Perception** may notice the cycle and call warnings, allowing enemies to react.
        
- **Wings of Flame (1/Day).** As an action, the wielder sprouts phoenix wings for 1 minute, gaining **60 ft fly speed**. Once during this effect, they can unleash a **wave of fire in a 30-foot cone** (Dexterity save DC 18, 6d8 fire damage).
    

---

## **Curses / Drawbacks**

- **Devotion’s Trial.** Each dawn, the wielder must succeed on a **DC 16 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, they lose access to the Feather’s powers for 24 hours.
    
- **Ashara’s Will.** The Feather resists selfish use. When the wielder acts out of cruelty or greed, the DM may call for a d20 roll (DC scaling with the severity: 12 → 15 → 18+). On a failure, the Feather lashes out, dealing **3d10 radiant or fire damage (DM’s choice)** to the wielder.
    

---

## **Destroying the Artifact**

The Feather of the Eternal Flame can only be destroyed by casting it into the **Primordial Flame** at the heart of Ashara’s sacred volcano during a phoenix’s rebirth cycle. Doing so consumes the relic but risks unleashing an uncontrollable **wildfire storm** across the desert, as the phoenix’s essence is violently torn from the world.

---

## **Lore**

Forged from the dying breath of Ashara Everdawn’s predecessor, the Feather was entrusted to the **Order of Eternal Flame** as both a weapon and symbol of balance. Its power helped end the **Age of Infernal Flames**, wielded by a chosen champion of Ashara.

Now, stolen by the **Sand Striders** under the command of **Kael Emberthorn**, the relic is due to be auctioned at a hidden oasis. The outcome of this event could decide whether the Feather becomes a beacon of renewal, a tool of oppression, or is destroyed forever.
