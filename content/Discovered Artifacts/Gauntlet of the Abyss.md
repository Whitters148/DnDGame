_Wondrous Item, artifact, requires attunement by a creature of strong will_

---

## **Description**

The **Gauntlet of the Abyss** is a dark, rune-etched relic that pulses with sickly green light. Abyssal whispers echo around it, promising power and ruin in equal measure. The metal is unnaturally cold, and when worn, it seems to dig into the flesh, entwining itself with the wielder’s very soul.

Legends claim it was forged by **Zar’vhal**, Demon Lord of the Abyss, to tempt mortals with a taste of his dominion. The Gauntlet amplifies the strength of its wielder but erodes their humanity, binding them ever closer to the darkness.

---

## **Properties**

While attuned to the Gauntlet, the wielder gains:

- **Strength of the Abyss.** Grants a **+2 bonus to Strength-based attack and damage rolls**, doubles carrying capacity, and provides **advantage on Strength checks and saving throws**.
    
- **Dark Resilience.** Grants **resistance to force and psychic damage**. As a reaction when taking force or psychic damage, the wielder may gain **temporary hit points equal to 10 + Constitution modifier**.
    
- **Abyssal Knowledge.** The wielder becomes fluent in **Abyssal** and intuitively understands fiendish rituals and dark magic.
    
- **Portal Creation (Recharge 3–4).** As an action, the wielder can open a small portal, teleporting themselves up to 60 feet to a space they can see. This works both in and out of combat.
    

---

## **Magical Abilities**

- **Abyssal Rifts (Recharge 5–6).** As an action, the wielder creates a 10-foot-radius rift within 60 feet, lasting until the end of their next turn. Creatures within 15 feet must make a **DC 18 Strength saving throw** or be pulled 10 feet toward it and restrained until the rift ends. On a failure by 5 or more, they also take **14 (4d6) necrotic damage**.
    
- **Summon Abyssal Echoes (1/Day).** The wielder summons **2d3 abyssal specters**, shadowy warriors bound by the Gauntlet’s power, that appear in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet. These creatures use the **Specter stat block** (*Monster Manual*), but their forms are twisted with abyssal runes and flickering green light. They remain for 1 hour or until destroyed, and they obey the wielder’s commands. However, if the wielder falls **unconscious, is incapacitated, or loses possession of the Gauntlet**, the Echoes immediately turn hostile to all creatures, attacking indiscriminately until destroyed.
    
- **Unholy Fortitude.** The wielder’s **Constitution score increases by +2** (maximum 24). If the wielder begins their turn with less than half their maximum hit points, they **regain 10 hit points**. This regeneration ceases for 1 round if the wielder takes **radiant damage**.
    

---

## **Curses / Drawbacks**

- **Abyssal Dependency.** At dawn, the wielder must succeed on a **DC 16 Wisdom saving throw**. On a failure, they suffer **disadvantage on all ability checks and saving throws** for the next 24 hours.
    
- **Soul-Binding Influence.** At the end of each day of attunement, the wielder must succeed on a **DC 14 Charisma saving throw**. On three cumulative failures, they are **permanently corrupted**, becoming a thrall of the Abyss. Their form warps monstrously, though redemption may still be possible through extraordinary means.
    

---

## **Destroying the Artifact**

The Gauntlet of the Abyss can only be destroyed by casting it into an **active Abyssal portal or gate**, then sealing the rift shut with the Gauntlet trapped inside. Only then will its bond to the mortal realm be broken, returning Zar’vhal’s essence to the Abyss.

---

## **Lore**

Forged by **Zar’vhal** himself, the Gauntlet was wielded by his generals during the Abyssal Wars to enslave kingdoms. Each mortal who bore it left devastation in their wake before being consumed by its corruption. Eventually, it was locked away by **Seraphina Blackthorn**, who guarded it at the Abyssal Gate.

Now in the possession of **Aizen Evander**, the Gauntlet tempts him with whispers of unimaginable power. Its fate may determine whether the Abyss is sealed forever — or unleashed upon Vaeloris.
