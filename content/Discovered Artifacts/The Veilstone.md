_Wondrous Item, artifact, requires attunement by a spellcaster or creature aligned with ley line magic_

---

## **Description**

The **Veilstone** is a smooth, black crystal that reflects no light, appearing as a void cut out of reality. It radiates a soul-deep cold and emanates a stillness so profound that time itself seems reluctant to move. Those who gaze upon it too long feel watched by something unseen, as though the Veil between worlds peers back.

Scholars believe it is a **fracture of the boundary between planes**, crystallised when the ley lines of Vaeloris first bent under divine war. To wield it is to touch a layer of existence not meant for mortal hands — a power both salvation and doom.

---

## **Properties**

While attuned to the Veilstone, the wielder gains:

- **Ley Nexus Conduit.** The Veilstone disrupts planar magic. Within 1 mile, spells of **7th level or lower** that involve teleportation or planar travel (_Teleport, Plane Shift, Etherealness_) automatically fail. Spells of higher levels require a **DC 20 Arcana check** to succeed. The wielder can also sense nearby **ley lines and distortions**, revealing hidden pathways or buried ruins.
    
- **Leyborn Sight.** The wielder experiences déjà vu and echoes of futures or pasts. Once per long rest, the wielder may **reroll a failed skill check or saving throw**. They also gain **advantage on Arcana checks** related to ley lines or planar phenomena.
    
- **Veil of Shadows.** The wielder exists slightly out of sync with reality. They gain **advantage on Stealth checks**, and creatures have **disadvantage on Perception checks** to notice them unless they have _truesight_.
    

---

## **Magical Abilities**

- **Phantom Step.** As a bonus action, the wielder may teleport up to **30 feet** through leyline distortions. If used to avoid an attack, the attack **automatically misses**.
    
- **Veil Breaker (Recharge 5–6).** The wielder shatters the veil, forcing enemies within 30 feet to glimpse alternate versions of themselves. Targets must make a **DC 18 Wisdom saving throw** or suffer the effects of the _Confusion_ spell for 1 minute. On success, a creature is **immune to this effect for 24 hours**.
    
- **Rift’s Edge (1/Day).** The wielder tears open a ley rift, dealing **8d10 force damage** in a 20-foot radius (Dexterity save DC 18 for half). On a failed save, targets are **phased into the void for one round**, skipping their next turn before reappearing **stunned until the end of their following turn**.
    

---

## **Severance Ability – Breaking Symbiotic Corruption**

The Veilstone is the **only known artifact capable of severing bonds of corruption or possession** — including Eryx’s symbiotes, Abyssal parasites, ghostly possession, or warlock pacts.

- **Initiation:** If the host is below **5% HP**, the Severance may begin instantly. Otherwise, the host must be **restrained or bound**.
    

### **The Severance Process (Cinematic Dice-Light Flow)**

1. **Channeling the Veilstone.** A Leyline Binding Field forms, freezing time as the host flickers between their true self and corrupted form.
    
2. **Breaking the Grip (Wisdom Contest).** DC 18 contested Wisdom saving throw vs. host.
    
    - Wielder success = bond weakens.
        
    - Symbiote success = resist, DC +2 for next step.
        
3. **Forcing Separation (Charisma Contest).** DC 18 contested Charisma saving throw vs. host.
    
    - Wielder success = control breaks further.
        
    - Symbiote success = lashes out, wielder and allies restraining take **3d8 psychic damage**.
        
4. **Final Severance.** DC 18 Wisdom or Charisma contest (host chooses which).
    
    - On success = symbiote expelled, vulnerable to destruction.
        
    - On failure = symbiote clings; the wielder must choose to **end the attempt** or **force it at a cost**.
        

### **Forcing Severance (Always a Cost)**

If the wielder pushes the Veilstone despite failure:

- Gain **1 level of exhaustion**.
    
- Take **4d6 psychic or force damage** (DM’s choice).
    
- Trigger **Leyline Instability**: a flare alerts Eryx or his lieutenants, or warps the area with hostile anomalies.
    

---

## **Curses / Drawbacks**

- **Existential Erosion.** Each dawn, the wielder must succeed on a **DC 16 Wisdom saving throw** or gain **1 level of exhaustion** as fragments of their mind drift between planes. Repeated failures may also induce **short-term madness**.
    
- **The Unseen’s Interest.** Prolonged use draws the notice of beings beyond the Veil. Whispers, alien dreams, or even stalking entities may manifest, growing more intrusive the longer it is wielded.
    
- **Divine Disconnect.** Healing spells on the wielder restore **only half their normal value**, and resurrection spells **automatically fail** unless performed within a Ley Nexus.
    

---

## **Destroying the Artifact**

The Veilstone cannot be broken by mortal tools. It must be cast into the **convergence point of three ley lines during a total eclipse**, sealed with a binding ritual. Doing so shatters it into fragments, but risks tearing open a permanent rift between worlds.

---

## **Lore**

The Veilstone is believed to be a fragment of the **original barrier between planes**, crystallised when the ley lines of Vaeloris were first scarred by primordial war. For centuries it lay dormant within **Mount Grimm**, avoided by mortals who feared its mind-breaking aura.

Now, whispers of its power have resurfaced. The Veilstone may be the only tool capable of freeing Eryx’s heralds from their corruption — or the weapon that finally unravels the fabric of Vaeloris.
