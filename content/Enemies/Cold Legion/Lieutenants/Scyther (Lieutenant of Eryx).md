### **Scyther (Lieutenant of Eryx)**

Huge aberration, neutral evil

**Once known as Vekrona Ironmind, an arcane prodigy, Scyther was consumed by her hunger for control over magic and reality. After bonding with the symbiote, she gained the ability to manipulate chaotic arcane forces, blending them with the biological power of her symbiote. Scyther is now a terrifying blend of magical prowess and biological might, capable of warping reality and turning the battlefield into a chaotic storm of magic and symbiotic tendrils.**

### **Innate Spellcasting**

Scyther’s innate spellcasting ability is **Intelligence** (spell save DC 19, +12 to hit with spell attacks). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- **At will**: _Eldritch Blast_, _Mage Hand_, _Detect Magic_, _Shield_
- **3/day each**: _Counterspell_, _Dispel Magic_, _Fireball_ (deals force damage instead of fire), _Chaos Bolt_
- **1/day each**: _Wall of Force_, _Finger of Death_, _Telekinesis_

---

### **Traits**

**Chaos-Symbiote Fusion**  
Scyther’s body is a seamless blend of arcane magic and biological symbiote power. Her limbs can morph into arcane tendrils, amplifying her spellcasting. As a bonus action, Scyther can enhance a spell she casts with symbiotic chaos, adding one of the following effects until the end of her turn:

- **Tendril Cast**: Extends the range of her spell by 30 feet.
- **Symbiotic Force**: Adds 2d8 force damage to any spell that deals damage.
- **Arcane Bind**: Enemies hit by her spells must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained by tendrils of chaotic magic until the end of their next turn.

**Symbiotic Regeneration**  
At the start of her turn, Scyther can regenerate 20 hit points. She does not regenerate if affected by radiant or thunder damage.

**Arcane Chaos**  
Scyther’s chaotic magic disrupts spellcasting around her. Any creature within 30 feet attempting to cast a spell must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or the spell fails, wasting the spell slot.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**  
Scyther can cast two cantrips or make two melee spell attacks with her arcane tendrils.

**Arcane Tendrils (Melee Spell Attack)**  
+12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 22 (4d6 + 6) force damage, and the target is pulled 10 feet toward Scyther.

**Symbiotic Grasp (Recharge 5–6)**  
Scyther summons her symbiotic magic, creating tendrils of dark energy. All creatures within a 30-foot radius must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or be entangled by these tendrils. Those who fail take 45 (10d8) necrotic damage and are restrained for 1 minute. A restrained creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Scyther can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Scyther regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

- **Arcane Hijack**: Scyther can choose a spell being cast within 60 feet and attempt to control it. The caster must make a DC 19 Charisma saving throw or have their spell redirected to a target of Scyther’s choice.
- **Symbiotic Charge**: Scyther uses her symbiotic limbs to channel arcane energy, replenishing her abilities. She regains one use of a spell she can cast innately (such as _Fireball_ or _Chaos Bolt_).
- **Chaos Pulse (Costs 2 Actions)**: Scyther releases a burst of chaotic magic in a 20-foot radius. Creatures in the area take 27 (6d8) force damage and are pushed back 15 feet.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Arcane Boost**  
_Scyther channels symbiotic energy into her spellcasting._  
As a bonus action, Scyther can add 2d6 force damage to the next spell she casts that deals damage.

**Tendril Cast**
_Scyther spawns her tendrils as an extension of her casting._
Extends the range of her spell by 30 feet

**Chaos Warp**  
_Scyther uses chaotic magic to teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space she can see._  
As a bonus action, Scyther teleports using her chaos magic.

---

### **Weaknesses**

Scyther’s regeneration is disabled when struck by radiant or thunder damage, preventing her from healing for 1 turn.

---

### **Backstory**

Once a brilliant arcane prodigy known as **Vekrona Ironmind**, her obsession with controlling magic and bending reality to her will drove her to investigate the rumors of the spreading corruption. Though her curiosity got the better of her and she found herself witnessing corruption firsthand. Detected by Myrk, she used her arcane abilities to attempt an escape however she was captured. Seeing great promise within this prodigy, Eryx decided to play god, merging magic with his own power. Though she resisted bonding at first, Vekrona was corrupted by the allure of ultimate control over forbidden and chaotic magic. Bonding with the symbiote granted her the ultimate fusion of magic and biological might, transforming her into a force of arcane chaos. Now, as **Scyther**, she commands the battlefield, warping reality and unleashing waves of destructive magic to further Eryx’s vision of dominance.
