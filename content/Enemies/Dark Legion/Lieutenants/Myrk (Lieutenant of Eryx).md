**Huge aberration, neutral evil**

Once a noble knight named **Darin Blackstone**, Myrk has become a deadly symbiotic hunter with heightened abilities, making him a master of stealth, shapeshifting, and tracking. Unlike most symbiotes, Myrk can manipulate his form to blend into any environment and disappear without a trace, making him the perfect predator. His symbiotic form grants him the ability to shift his limbs into deadly weapons and track his prey across vast distances. Myrk’s advanced abilities make him one of Eryx’s most effective enforcers, blending power with stealth.

### **Traits**

**Advanced Shapeshifting**:  
Myrk can alter his symbiotic form to gain combat advantages or conceal himself. As a bonus action, he can reshape his body to create one of the following effects:

- **Combat Appendages**: He gains an extra attack on his turn using a new limb (claw or tendril).
- **Camouflage**: Myrk becomes invisible for 1 minute, even in bright light, or until he attacks or takes damage.
- **Shifting Blades**: Myrk’s melee attacks deal an additional 1d10 slashing damage until the start of his next turn.

**Symbiotic Tracking**:  
Myrk can track any creature he has encountered by honing in on its unique biological signature. As a bonus action, he can sense the location of a creature within 1 mile that he has previously fought, even through magical invisibility or teleportation effects.

**Symbiotic Regeneration**:  
Myrk regenerates 15 hit points at the start of his turn. He does not regenerate if he takes radiant or fire damage.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**:  
Myrk makes three attacks with his claws or tendrils.

**Tendril Strike**:  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +14 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 23 (4d6 + 7) slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be grappled (escape DC 19).

**Claw Swipe**:  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 25 (4d8 + 7) slashing damage.

**Shadow Ambush (Recharge 5–6)**:  
Myrk disappears into the shadows and reappears behind a target. The target must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 35 (10d6) necrotic damage and become stunned until the end of its next turn.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Myrk can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Myrk regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Tendril Slash**:  
Myrk makes a tendril strike against a creature within 15 feet of him.

**Disappear**:  
Myrk turns invisible and moves up to his speed.

**Symbiotic Surge (Costs 2 Actions)**:  
Myrk surges with symbiotic energy, gaining temporary hit points equal to 30 (5d12) and increasing his speed by 20 feet until the end of his next turn.

### **Weaknesses**:

Myrk’s regeneration is disabled by radiant or fire damage, preventing him from healing for 1 turn.

### **Backstory**

Once a noble knight of the Frozen North, **Darin Blackstone** was a legendary tracker and hunter. His code of honor and skills on the battlefield were renowned. But his thirst for ultimate power and knowledge led him down a dark path, seeking out the forbidden powers of **Eryx**. Bonded with a symbiote, Darin was reborn as **Myrk**, a master of stealth and death. Now serving Eryx, Myrk uses his newfound abilities to hunt his prey and eliminate all those who dare resist his master’s will.
