Large aberration, chaotic evil

**Once a barbarian named Selene Ashenclaw, Raze bonded with a symbiote that amplified her bloodthirsty tendencies, turning her into a destructive enforcer of chaos. Her symbiote powers allow her to use long, whip-like tendrils to strike enemies, blending primal fury with unpredictable combat tactics. Now, as one of Eryx's most dangerous lieutenants, she embodies chaos and destruction, striking fear into all who oppose her.**

### **Traits**

**Tendril Hair**:  
Raze’s long, whip-like tendrils extend from her head, capable of striking foes at range. She can make melee attacks with these tendrils that have a 15 ft. reach and can grapple or restrain enemies.

**Symbiotic Regeneration**:  
When Raze takes damage, she regains 10 hit points at the start of her turn as long as she has taken damage in the last round. This ability does not function if she is affected by fire or radiant damage.

**Primal Chaos**:  
Raze thrives in chaotic combat. When she is surrounded by more than three enemies, her attacks deal an additional 2d6 slashing damage as she revels in the chaos of battle.

**Camouflage**:  
Raze can use her symbiote to blend into her surroundings, becoming nearly invisible. She can move stealthily, gaining advantage on Stealth checks when in dim light or darkness.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**  
Raze makes three attacks with her tendrils or claws.

**Tendril Strike**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 21 (3d8 + 7) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be restrained by the tendril (escape DC 18).

**Claw Strike**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 24 (4d6 + 7) slashing damage.

**Rage Swipe (Recharge 5–6)**  
Raze makes a wide sweep with her tendrils, striking all creatures within 10 feet of her. Each target must succeed on a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 30 (6d8 + 7) slashing damage and be knocked prone.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Raze can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Raze regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

**Tendril Lash**: Raze makes one tendril strike against a creature within 15 feet of her.

**Symbiotic Fury**: Raze heals for 20 hit points and gains resistance to all damage (except psychic) until the start of her next turn.

**Unleash Chaos (Costs 2 Actions)**: Raze whips her tendrils in all directions, forcing all enemies within 20 feet to make a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked back 10 feet and take 22 (4d10) slashing damage.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Tendril Swipe**  
_Raze quickly strikes with her tendrils, overwhelming her enemies._  
Raze makes a tendril strike as a bonus action (reach 15 ft.).

**Symbiotic Rage**  
_Raze fuels her fury with symbiotic power, gaining temporary strength._  
As a bonus action, Raze gains temporary hit points equal to 20 (3d10) until the start of her next turn.

---

### **Weaknesses**

Raze’s regenerative healing is halted when she takes fire or radiant damage, preventing her from healing for 1 round.

---

### **Backstory**

Once a fierce barbarian known as **Selene Ashenclaw**, she put up a great fight on the front lines defending her village. This determination and drive was something special Eryx hadn't seen in many others. He realised she was too good to waste as a simple thrall, and chose to bond her with Raze. Raze’s lust for battle and destruction amplified Selene's barbaric nature to even greater levels. Her bond with the symbiote under **Eryx**'s influence turned her into a chaotic enforcer, reveling in violence and unpredictability. Now, as Raze, she is a living force of destruction, embodying primal fury and chaos, striking down those who dare resist Eryx's dark ambitions.
