Large aberration, lawful evil

**Once a decorated military commander named Ramon Frostweaver, Tendrath has become a living weapon within Eryx’s army. His corrupted symbiotic form grants him control over multiple tendrils that extend from his body, making him a brutal and efficient combatant. Tendrath serves as the leader of the Cold Legion and uses his deadly control over tendrils to dominate enemies in battle.**

**Armor Class**: 18 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points**: 280 (25d10 + 150)  
**Speed**: 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|22 (+6)|18 (+4)|20 (+5)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|17 (+3)|

**Saving Throws**: Str +12, Dex +10, Con +11  
**Skills**: Athletics +12, Intimidation +9, Stealth +10  
**Damage Resistances**: Cold, Necrotic  
**Damage Immunities**: Poison  
**Condition Immunities**: Poisoned, Frightened  
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages**: Abyssal, Common, telepathy 60 ft.  
**Challenge**: 18 (20,000 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus**: +6

---

### **Traits**

**Symbiotic Control**:  
Tendrath can control the tendrils from his body with immense precision. He gains an additional attack when using his tendrils in combat.

**Tendril Regeneration**:  
Tendrath regains 20 hit points at the start of his turn. He does not regenerate if he takes radiant or fire damage.

**Enhanced Tendril Manipulation**:  
Tendrath can use his tendrils to interact with objects or manipulate the environment. He gains advantage on Athletics checks when using his tendrils for these purposes.

**Web Tendrils**:  
As an action, Tendrath can shoot web-like tendrils within a 20-foot radius, forcing creatures in the area to make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or be restrained (escape DC 18).

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**  
Tendrath makes three attacks: two with his tendrils and one with his claw or bite.

**Tendril Strike**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +12 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 21 (3d8 + 6) slashing damage. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be grappled (escape DC 18). While grappled, the target is restrained.

**Claw Slash**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 23 (4d6 + 6) slashing damage.

**Tendril Whip**  
_Ranged Weapon Attack_: +12 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 19 (3d6 + 6) slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be pulled 10 feet toward Tendrath.

**Tendril Frenzy (Recharge 5–6)**
Tendrath lashes out with all of his tendrils in a wide area, striking multiple foes at once. All creatures within a 15-foot radius must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, they take 30 (6d6) slashing damage and are knocked prone. On a successful save, they take half damage and remain standing.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Tendrath can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Tendrath regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Tendril Slash**  
Tendrath makes a tendril strike against a creature within 15 feet of him.

**Crushing Grasp**  
Tendrath uses his tendrils to grapple a creature within 15 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or take 25 (4d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage and be restrained.

**Symbiotic Burst (Costs 2 Actions)**  
Tendrath surges with symbiotic power, gaining temporary hit points equal to 30 (6d10), and his speed increases by 10 feet until the end of his next turn.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Symbiotic Finesse**
_Tendrath uses his symbiotic form to deftly maneuver or disrupt his foes._  
Tendrath gains advantage on his next attack roll, or he can impose disadvantage on one enemy's attack roll against him until the start of his next turn.

**Weapon Shift**  
_Tendrath morphs his symbiotic weapon for combat versatility._  
As a bonus action, Tendrath can shift his tendrils into a different weapon (bladed, spiked, or whip-like tendrils), changing the damage type of his next attack to slashing, piercing, or bludgeoning.

**Tendril Grapple**  
_Tendrath uses his tendrils to quickly ensnare a target._  
Tendrath attempts to grapple a creature within 15 feet. On a failed DC 18 Strength save, the target is grappled.

---

### **Weaknesses**

Tendrath's regeneration is disabled by psychic or fire damage for 1 turn.

---

### **Backstory**:

**Ramon Frostweaver** was once a decorated military commander from the Frozen North, known for his tactical brilliance and cold, calculated strategies. Seeking greater power, Ramon willingly bonded with a symbiote under Eryx’s influence, transforming him into **Tendrath**. Now the leader of the **Cold Legion**, Tendrath is a living weapon driven by a need for dominance and control. His inner conflict between his former disciplined mind and the symbiote’s primal hunger makes him an unpredictable and deadly force on the battlefield. Tendrath’s ultimate goal is to prove his superiority by crushing all who stand in his way, including his fellow lieutenants.
