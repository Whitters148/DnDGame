Huge aberration, Neutral evil

**Once known as Garron Stonebreaker, Thrashor has become the ultimate enforcer of brute strength under Eryx's command. His symbiotic form transforms him into a towering juggernaut capable of smashing enemies with immense force. His limbs shift into heavy weaponry, making him an unstoppable force on the battlefield.**

**Armor Class**: 22 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points**: 320 (25d12 + 200)  
**Speed**: 40 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 26 (+8) | 14 (+2) | 24 (+7) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) |

**Saving Throws**: Str +14, Dex +8, Con +13, Wis +7   
**Skills**: Athletics +14, Intimidation +8, Perception +7  
**Damage Resistances**: Cold, Necrotic  
**Damage Immunities**: Poison  
**Condition Immunities**: Poisoned, Frightened  
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 17  
**Languages**: Abyssal, Common  
**Challenge**: 20 (25,000 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus**: +6

---

### **Traits**

**Brutal Weaponry**:  
Thrashor can morph his limbs into warhammers, battleaxes, or mauls, dealing heavy damage. His melee attacks deal an additional 1d12 damage.

**Juggernaut Charge**:  
If Thrashor moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a melee attack, the target takes an extra 10 (3d6) damage and must make a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Symbiotic Regeneration**:  
Thrashor regains 15 hit points at the start of his turn unless he takes fire or radiant damage.

**Heavy Fortification**:  
Thrashor’s massive frame makes him resistant to forceful effects. He has advantage on saving throws against being pushed or knocked prone.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**  
Thrashor makes two attacks with his warhammer or axe-shaped limbs.

**Warhammer Smash**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 24 (3d10 + 8) bludgeoning damage.

**Axe Strike**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 22 (3d8 + 8) slashing damage.

**Symbiotic Slam (Recharge 5–6)**  
Thrashor slams his symbiotic limbs into the ground, creating a shockwave. All creatures within 20 feet must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw or take 35 (6d10) bludgeoning damage and be stunned until the end of their next turn.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Thrashor can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Thrashor regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Brutal Strike**  
Thrashor makes a melee attack with his warhammer or axe.

**Crushing Charge**  
Thrashor moves up to his speed and makes a melee attack. If he hits, the target is knocked prone.

**Symbiotic Burst (Costs 2 Actions)**  
Thrashor surges with symbiotic power, gaining 40 (5d12) temporary hit points and increasing his speed by 10 feet until the end of his next turn.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Weapon Morph**  
_Thrashor quickly shifts his symbiotic form into a new heavy weapon._  
As a bonus action, Thrashor can change his weapon (warhammer, axe, or maul). The new weapon grants an additional 1d12 damage on the next attack.

**Battering Charge**  
_Thrashor surges forward with brute force._  
Thrashor moves up to half his speed. If he moves at least 10 feet, his next melee attack deals an extra 2d6 damage.

**Symbiotic Block**  
_Thrashor hardens his symbiotic limbs to absorb a blow._  
As a bonus action, Thrashor gains +2 AC until the start of his next turn.

---

### **Weaknesses**

Thrashor's regeneration is disabled by fire or radiant damage for 1 turn.

---

### **Backstory**:

**Garron Stonebreaker** was once a renowned warrior of the northern tribes, who now works as a smith providing troops with weapons and armour they require. His physical strength and combat prowess made him a deadly mercenary, feared across the Frozen North. However, things went bad when a contract was ordered against someone who had been corrupted. After underestimating his target he was left bloodied in the snow and ice, clinging on to the last bit of life he had. Until his life was spared and was offered a second chance at life by Eryx. He wasted no time and bonded with the symbiote that would transform him into **Thrashor**. Now a living weapon, he serves as Eryx's enforcer, using his brutal strength to crush any who oppose his master. Thrashor’s philosophy is simple: **might makes right**.
