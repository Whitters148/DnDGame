Medium humanoid (elf), neutral evil

_Kaida Shadowsong is a master of silent elimination, her lethal movements wielded as a weapon of terror under Lord Damaris’ command._

**Appearance**: Kaida is a striking figure, with alabaster skin and sharp, elven features that exude an eerie elegance. Her long, jet-black hair is styled into a sleek braid that cascades down her back. Her piercing violet eyes are intense and calculating, standing out against her pale complexion. She wears expertly crafted black leather armor, fitted closely to her form and accented with intricate silver detailing, blending elegance with deadly functionality. Around her neck rests a glowing purple gemstone, which pulses faintly with a dark energy, matching the smaller gems embedded in her armor. Her pointed ears, poised expression, and graceful stance give her a refined, almost regal aura, while her demeanor radiates a controlled and lethal intensity.

**Armor Class**: 17 (Studded Leather, magically enhanced)  
**Hit Points**: 190 (25d8 + 75)  
**Speed**: 40 ft.

### **Traits**

**Shadow Step (2/day)**  
As a bonus action, Kaida can teleport up to 120 ft. to an unoccupied space she can see that is in dim light or darkness.

**Evasion**  
If Kaida is subjected to an effect that allows her to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, she instead takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.

**Uncanny Dodge**  
When an attacker Kaida can see hits her with an attack, she can use her reaction to halve the damage.

**Veil of Shadows (1/day)**  
As an action, Kaida can become invisible in dim light or darkness for 1 minute or until she attacks, casts a spell, or enters bright light.

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**  
Kaida makes three attacks with her Shadow Dagger or two with her Hand Crossbow.

**Shadow Dagger**  
_Melee Weapon Attack_: +11 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 9 (1d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) poison damage.

- **Shadow Strike**: If Kaida makes this attack while in dim light or darkness, the target takes an additional 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. If the target is killed by this attack, they dissolve into shadows, leaving no trace.
- **Venomous Bite**: On a successful hit, the target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned, the target takes 7 (2d6) poison damage at the start of each of its turns. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each turn to end the effect.

**Hand Crossbow**  
_Ranged Weapon Attack_: +11 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.  
_Hit_: 9 (1d6 + 6) piercing damage.

**Shadow Dance**  
Kaida teleports up to 30 ft., makes a melee attack with her Shadow Dagger, and then teleports up to 30 ft. again as a single action.

**Poison Cloud (1/day)**  
Kaida throws a vial of poison, creating a 15-foot-radius cloud. Each creature within the area must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or take 14 (4d6) poison damage and be poisoned for 1 minute. A poisoned target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

Kaida can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. Kaida regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

- **Shadow Strike**: Kaida makes one attack with her Shadow Dagger.
- **Vanish**: Kaida moves up to her speed and can attempt to hide as long as she is in dim light or darkness.
- **Night Terror (Costs 2 Actions)**: Kaida forces one creature within 30 ft. that can see her to make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

**Shadow Veil**  
Kaida shrouds herself in shadows, granting advantage on Stealth checks until the end of her next turn.

---

### **Reactions**

**Evasive Step**  
When a creature moves within 10 feet of Kaida, she can use her reaction to step back up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. If she ends this movement in dim light or darkness, she can attempt a Stealth check to hide.

**Shadow Counter**  
When a creature misses Kaida with a melee attack, she can make one melee attack with her Shadow Dagger against that creature as a reaction. If the attack hits, the creature must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or become poisoned until the end of its next turn.

**Retaliatory Venom**  
If Kaida takes damage from a creature within 5 feet, she can use her reaction to unleash a burst of poisonous mist. The attacker must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned, the creature takes 5 (1d10) poison damage at the start of each of its turns. It can repeat the saving throw at the end of each turn to end the effect.

---

### **Lair Actions**

On initiative count 20 (losing ties), Kaida can take one of the following lair actions. She can’t use the same action two rounds in a row.

- **Veil of Darkness**: Shadows deepen within a 30-foot radius centered on Kaida, making the area heavily obscured for 1 round. Nonmagical light cannot illuminate the area, and creatures other than Kaida have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks to see.
    
- **Dark Passage**: A shadowy doorway appears at a location Kaida can see within 60 feet. She can immediately teleport to this location as a reaction, avoiding opportunity attacks and gaining automatic success on any Stealth check she attempts afterward.
    
- **Creeping Dread**: One creature within 60 feet that can see Kaida must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened until the end of its next turn. While frightened, the creature sees shadowy versions of Kaida flickering in its peripheral vision, causing it to have disadvantage on attack rolls.
    
- **Poisonous Haze**: A thin, nearly invisible mist fills a 20-foot-radius area within 60 feet of Kaida. Each creature in the area must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) poison damage at the start of its turn while in the haze. The haze dissipates at the end of the next round.
    

---

### **Weaknesses**

**Sunlight Sensitivity**  
Kaida has disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight when she, the target, or the object being perceived is in direct sunlight.

**Eternal Darkness**  
Kaida cannot regain hit points while in direct sunlight and has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws if exposed to bright light for over 1 hour.

**Insidious Whispers**  
Once per day, if Kaida fails a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw at the start of a long rest, she gains 2 levels of exhaustion and feels a compulsion to harm or sabotage an ally.

---

### **Backstory**

Raised within the deadly and intricate society of the elven elite, Kaida’s natural talent for stealth and assassination made her a feared figure even among her own kind. Drawn to the order and control promised by Lord Damaris, she accepted a position as his enforcer. Through her legendary Shadow Dagger, Kaida weaves fear and death into every encounter, her heart colder with each kill, serving as both the Oppressors' deadliest weapon and a looming threat in the shadows.
