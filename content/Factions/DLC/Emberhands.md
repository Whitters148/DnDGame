- **Type**: Underground Resistance
- **Region**: Newtonia and its borderlands
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Good (public face); some internal elements drift toward Chaotic Neutral
- **Symbol**: A handprint scorched into wood, parchment, or stone — subtle and heat-scorched
- **Motto**: _"Truth in flame. Chains in ash."_

---

### **Overview**

The Emberhands are a decentralised network of artists, rebels, smugglers, and saboteurs who fight against King Vallion Drexmere’s authoritarian regime in Newtonia. Born from the ashes of Quandavious Dingle Snoot III’s betrayal and disappearance, they operate through coded performances, enchanted scripts, smuggled letters, and arcane graffiti.

They’re often mistaken for scattered dissidents, but beneath the surface lies military coordination, magical encryption, and deep loyalty to their cause—and to the legend of QDS.

---

### **Structure**

- **Ashwalkers**: Smugglers and saboteurs who guide people and secrets through the underground (Whispercrypts, sewer routes, etc.).
    
- **Kindlers**: Poets, playwrights, and bards spreading dissent through enchanted or metaphor-laced performances.
    
- **Sootblades**: Militant street cells — ex-soldiers, ex-guards, and vigilantes who strike strategic targets (Ironcloak Watchposts, propaganda archives, etc.).
    
- **The Hollow Flame**: A secretive inner circle rumoured to be in contact with QDS himself. Many believe it’s myth. Only Captain Velra is known to operate at this level.
    

---

### **Notable Leader: Captain Velra Ardineth**

- **Race/Class**: Half-Elf Ranger/Rogue (Gloom Stalker & Inquisitive)
    
- **Role**: Operational head of Emberhand resistance cells
    
- **Traits**: Quiet, strategic, deadly. Protective of younger members. Former Royal Hunter turned fugitive.
    
- **Relationship with QDS**: Bond of mutual respect and ideological friction. She is the flame that carries the legend forward.
    

---

### **Goals**

- Uncover and reveal the truth behind the Pale Accord and King Vallion’s rise to power
    
- Restore free expression, art, and magical freedom in Newtonia
    
- Keep the memory (and mission) of QDS alive
    
- Disrupt Ironcloak operations and liberate controlled districts
    

---

### **Player Hooks**

- **Ally Contact**: The party may be approached by Emberhands offering cryptic quests or hidden performances.
    
- **Propaganda Recovery**: Players might find censored performances or the true ledger QDS created.
    
- **Velra Missions**: Infiltrate, recover, or sabotage — always with symbolic weight.
    
- **False Allegiances**: Players could be mistaken as traitors or undercover agents, forcing tense social conflict.
    
- **QDS Recognition**: If the players ever perform his work or speak his name, they risk Ironcloak attention.
