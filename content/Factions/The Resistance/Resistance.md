_“So long as we stand, the world still has hope.”_

---

## Overview

The Resistance is a powerful and public coalition of warriors, mystics, scholars, and civilians united against the tyrannical reign of Lord Damaris and his Oppressors.  
What began as scattered uprisings has coalesced into a central force for good, bolstered by legendary leaders and an organised network of wings dedicated to freedom across Vaeloris.

They are survivors, defenders, and the unwavering **light in the dark**. Though once fractured and outnumbered, the tide is beginning to turn. The Resistance now rallies allies, unites fractured factions, and reignites hope where once there was only silence.

---

## Goals / Beliefs / Creed

- **Hope** – Inspire the people even in the bleakest moments.
    
- **Unity** – Many races, cultures, and creeds fighting as one.
    
- **Justice** – Stand not for revenge, but for what is right.
    
- **Sacrifice** – Every victory is hard-earned; the fallen are honoured.
    
- **Truth** – Speak what the Oppressors silence, reveal lies, and spark defiance.
    

---

## Typical Activities

- Training and supplying armed forces in liberated lands.
    
- Coordinating sabotage, assassinations, and espionage behind enemy lines.
    
- Protecting towns and villages from abyssal corruption and Oppressor reprisals.
    
- Restoring hope through speeches, rituals, and symbols of unity.
    

---

## Membership

- Members include soldiers, clerics, arcane scholars, common folk, and exiles.
    
- Recruitment is open to all who oppose the Oppressors; each wing trains its own specialists.
    
- Symbols vary by wing, but most fighters bear the sigil of the phoenix — a reminder of survival and rebirth.
    
- Membership is both honour and burden: service comes with risk, secrecy, and sacrifice.
    

---

## Ranks & Hierarchy

The Resistance is governed by a **High Command**, supported by specialised wings. Common progression:

- **Initiate** – Newly sworn member.
    
- **Operative** – Proven agent trusted with missions.
    
- **Commander** – Leads a unit or wing detachment.
    
- **High Commander** – Governs a full wing.
    
- **Council Seat** – Member of High Command itself.
    

---

## Allies & Rivals

- **Allies**: Neutral regions sympathetic to their cause, independent mages, outlawed nobles, and factions seeking freedom from the Oppressors.
    
- **Rivals**: Lord Damaris’ forces, abyssal cults, and mercenaries who profit from tyranny.
    

The Oppressors see the Resistance not merely as rebels but as a **disease of dissent** — a threat to their vision of a “perfectly united world.”

## Quests & Adventure Hooks

- Escort civilians or leaders through Oppressor-controlled territory.
    
- Retrieve stolen relics or magical artefacts before the Oppressors corrupt them.
    
- Infiltrate and sabotage Oppressor supply lines.
    
- Protect liberated villages from retribution.
    
- Resolve tensions between wings with conflicting methods.
    

---

## DM Notes

- The Resistance should feel **inspirational but costly** — every victory carries a price.
    
- Leaders like Arlon Stormcrest and Elder Mordwyn embody hope and burden in equal measure.
    
- Tone is **defiant yet grounded**: heroes are flawed but resolute.
    
- Mechanically, use the **Renown system** to represent advancement within the faction, with boons tied to each wing.
