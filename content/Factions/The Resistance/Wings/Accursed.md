## Overview

The **Accursed** are the [[Resistance]]’s most unsettling wing — a handpicked fellowship of cursed, monstrous, and supernatural operatives. Each bears a gift that others would call a blight: lycanthropy, necromancy, witchcraft, spectral communion, or stranger, darker powers.

Distrusted by most and spoken of in whispers, the Accursed are nonetheless indispensable. They wield the uncanny against the abyss, confronting threats no ordinary soldier could survive.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Weaponise curses and monstrous gifts for the Resistance cause.
    
- Confront supernatural threats beyond the Hollow Circle’s reach.
    
- Embrace powers others fear, using them as tools of survival and victory.
    
- Remain a **necessary evil** — fighting darkness with darkness.
    

---

## Activities

- Hunts against abyssal horrors, undead, and supernatural foes.
    
- Commanding the dead, binding spirits, or taming monstrous forms.
    
- Executing missions too dangerous for mortal troops.
    
- Supporting High Command with occult force when secrecy or subtlety is not enough.

## Allies & Rivalries

- **Emberblades** see them as dangerously unpredictable.
    
- **Whisper Network** occasionally uses them as cover legends or to sow fear.
    
- **Frostguard** distrust their methods but grudgingly admit their utility.
    
- **Circle of Light** provides cleansing when corruption edges too far.
    
- **Hollow Circle** respect them as kin, but argue over how far into the dark is too far.
    

---

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs must work with an Accursed operative on a mission where only their powers suffice.
    
- A Resistance town revolts at the sight of an Accursed warrior — the party must mediate.
    
- An Accursed goes rogue, consumed by their curse; the PCs are tasked to stop or redeem them.
    
- The party discovers a new cursed recruit undergoing initiation — and must decide whether to trust them.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **unsettling, uncanny, necessary evil.**
    
- The Accursed should make allies uneasy but indispensable.
    
- They blur the line between **ally and monster**, offering strong RP and moral dilemmas.
