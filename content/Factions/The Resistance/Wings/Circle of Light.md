_“The Light walks with you.”_

---

## Overview

The **Circle of Light** is the Resistance’s **arcane and divine bastion** — a sacred fellowship of ritualists, healers, and wardweavers devoted to protecting the soul of the movement.

Where the Emberblades strike and the Frostguard defends, the Circle shields, restores, and pushes back against abyssal corruption through light, sacred knowledge, and ritual. They are the **guardians of life**, both physical and spiritual — often overlooked, but utterly indispensable.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Preserve the spiritual and magical health of the Resistance.
    
- Shield allies with divine wards and arcane protections.
    
- Cleanse corruption from individuals and artefacts.
    
- Offer spiritual rites to honour the fallen.
    
- Sustain morale through ritual and guidance.
    

---

## Activities

- Establishing and defending sanctums at leyline sites and reclaimed ruins.
    
- Healing wounded soldiers and civilians in Resistance territories.
    
- Performing cleansing rites for cursed or abyssal-tainted relics.
    
- Maintaining magical communication networks.
    
- Supporting other wings with protective wards and resurrection rituals.
    

---

## Membership

- Members include clerics, abjurers, wardweavers, and ritualists.
    
- **Symbol**: a rising sun within a protective ring of runes.
    
- Rituals include blessings whispered before battle (_“The Light walks with you”_), starlight candle cleansings for cursed items, and the **Ceremony of the Fallen** to lay the dead to rest uncorrupted.
    
- Initiates undergo the **Trial of Reflection**, confronting their own inner darkness before swearing service.
    

---

## Ranks & Structure

- **Warden**: [[Kara Thornveil]] – calm, composed, spiritually anchored leader.
    
- **Senior Lightbinders** – veteran wardmasters and divine casters.
    
- **Sanctum Keepers** – regional heads of temples and field sanctuaries.
    
- **Lightwardens** – trained casters serving as combat healers and warders.

---

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs are tasked to **defend a Circle sanctum** against a sudden abyssal incursion.
    
- An artefact arrives corrupted — the Circle calls on the party to recover missing ritual components for cleansing.
    
- A **Trial of Reflection** goes wrong, unleashing inner demons into the world; PCs must contain them.
    
- A Circle operative has disappeared on a mission with crucial magical knowledge.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **protective, ritualistic, compassionate.**
    
- The Circle provides opportunities for **healing, roleplay, and moral dilemmas** rather than raw combat.
    
- Sanctums are excellent locations for respite, magical rituals, or story-based corruption-cleansing quests.
