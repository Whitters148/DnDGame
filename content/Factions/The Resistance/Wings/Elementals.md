## Overview

The **Elementals** are the [[Resistance]]’s most unpredictable and versatile wing — a rare collection of operatives attuned to the primal forces of Vaeloris. Each member channels a unique element as their power: fire, water, earth, air, lightning, metal, plants, glass, even blood.

They are admired for their raw strength but feared for their volatility. When unleashed, they can turn the tide of battle in ways no other force can, embodying both creation and destruction.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Harness elemental gifts to heal, destroy, or reshape the battlefield.
    
- Provide the Resistance with versatility where conventional forces fail.
    
- Preserve and weaponise ancient traditions of elemental attunement.
    
- Inspire both awe and terror in allies and enemies alike.
    

---

## Activities

- Devastating elemental strikes in mass combat.
    
- Shaping terrain to fortify, collapse, or flood battlefields.
    
- Healing allies or shielding civilians with elemental wards.
    
- Supporting rescue operations, evacuations, and fortifications.
    
- Undertaking elite missions requiring their unique gifts.

## Allies & Rivalries

- **Emberblades** respect their destructive power but view them as unpredictable.
    
- **Whisper Network** considers them difficult to conceal, yet invaluable for diversions.
    
- **Frostguard** admire their battlefield utility and often deploy alongside them.
    
- **Circle of Light** treat them as natural allies in the struggle against corruption.
    
- **Hollow Circle** remain wary of elemental corruption, likening it to abyssal taint.
    

---

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs are tasked to escort an Elemental during a mission requiring their unique abilities.
    
- An Elementalist loses control, threatening a Resistance village — the party must stop them.
    
- Oppressor forces capture an Elemental to exploit their power; the PCs must mount a rescue.
    
- The Trial of Balance begins, and the party is invited to witness or intervene.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **mythic spectacle, volatile power.**
    
- Use Elementals to create cinematic moments: collapsing mountains, summoning storms, flooding fields.
    
- They should feel like **living legends**, called in sparingly but decisively.
