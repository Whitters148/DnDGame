## Overview

The **Emberblades** are the most feared operatives of the Resistance. Drawn from assassins, saboteurs, mercenaries, and military defectors, they specialise in **covert strikes, targeted killings, and strategic disruption**.

Operating in **small, surgical units**, the Emberblades strike where stealth, brutality, and psychological warfare will do more damage than armies. Their missions are **high-risk, high-reward**, often skirting moral lines. They do not fight for glory — only to win.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Eliminate high-value targets.
    
- Disrupt Oppressor infrastructure and supply lines.
    
- Spread fear and uncertainty behind enemy lines.
    
- Extract key Resistance operatives or defectors.
    
- Ensure the Oppressors never feel secure.
    

---

## Activities

- Sabotaging bridges, siege engines, and magical constructs.
    
- Infiltrating fortresses, mage towers, and occupied cities.
    
- Coordinating assassinations of generals, sorcerers, or commanders.
    
- Conducting precision rescues of captured Resistance members.
    
- Offering personal contracts to trusted allies or adventurers.
    

---

## Membership

- Emberblades are recruited from the **most ruthless and capable**: ex-sellswords, assassins, defected soldiers, and Resistance loyalists willing to forsake mercy.
    
- Most abandon their true names, known only by codenames or unit designations.
    
- Their **symbol** is a black blade surrounded by fading red embers, often carried as a token.
    
- Operatives swear the _Oath of the Ember_ before missions:
    
    > _“No light. No fear. No failure.”_
    

---

## Ranks & Structure

- **Commander**: [[Kaelen Darkspear]] – rules with efficiency; results above all.
    
- **Tactical Lieutenants** – regional field leaders (DM may create).
    
- **Strike Teams** – specialised squads with distinct reputations:
    
    - _Ashborn Unit_ – infiltration and covert sabotage.
        
    - _Wraithfire Cell_ – master assassins and killers.
        
    - _Silent Cinders_ – leave no trace of their work.

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs are offered a **black contract** by Kaelor: a high-risk assassination behind enemy lines.
    
- An Emberblade cell has gone missing in an Oppressor city; the party is sent to recover them.
    
- Whisper Network intelligence suggests Kaelor acted without approval — the PCs must investigate.
    
- Rumours claim one Emberblade has **betrayed the Resistance**; the party must uncover the truth.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **brutal, surgical, feared.**
    
- Emberblades can serve as NPC allies, rivals, or uneasy patrons for the party.
    
- Their missions are ideal for morally grey or stealth-focused adventures.
    
- Only a handful of the **original Emberblades** are said to survive, adding to their mystique.
