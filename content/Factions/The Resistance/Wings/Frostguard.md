## Overview

The **Frostguard** are the Resistance’s **primary military force** — a disciplined, battle-hardened army forged in the unforgiving wilds of the Frozen North.

Led by [[Jarl Hrovar Ironhelm]], the Frostguard embody resilience and tactical precision. Where the Emberblades strike in shadows and the Circle wards the spirit, the Frostguard hold the line. They are deployed across every Resistance-controlled territory to defend settlements, secure leylines, and meet Oppressor armies head-on.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Fortify and defend key Resistance strongholds.
    
- Secure leyline crossings and strategic chokepoints.
    
- Escort caravans, refugees, and dignitaries safely across Vaeloris.
    
- Serve as the Resistance’s first line of defence against Oppressor incursions.
    
- Train militias and civilian recruits into capable defenders.
    

---

## Activities

- Stationing garrisons in Resistance-aligned towns and villages.
    
- Escorting supply lines and refugee caravans.
    
- Patrolling contested wilderness zones to intercept Oppressor scouts or corrupted monsters.
    
- Reinforcing other wings with sheer numbers and battlefield discipline.
    
- Providing the party with military support or reinforcements in major conflicts.
    

---

## Membership

- Recruits are drawn from Frostgale and other northern strongholds, though any proven warrior may join.
    
- Members are hardened by endurance trials and survival in the cold.
    
- **Symbol**: a mountain peak encircled by a frost-rimmed greatshield.
    
- **Trial of Cold Steel**: a brutal week-long survival test that all recruits endure.
    
- Frostguard units are renowned for their training against elemental hazards, abyssal corruption, and battlefield attrition.
    

---

## Ranks & Structure

- **Warden**: [[Jarl Hrovar Ironhelm]] – disciplined commander, loyal to Arlon Stormcrest.
    
- **Field Captains** – lead regional units and deployments.
    
- **Frostguard Units**:
    
    - _Iceclad Legion_ – heavy infantry.
        
    - _Frostveil Scouts_ – rangers and wilderness trackers.
        
    - _Stormbearers_ – siege soldiers and magical artillery.
        
    - _Shieldwroughts_ – elite bodyguards for Resistance leaders and sanctums.

## Allies & Rivalries

|Faction|Relationship|
|---|---|
|**Emberblades**|Respectful but distant — Frostguard endure the fallout while Emberblades vanish.|
|**Whisper Network**|Tense cooperation — Frostguard demand intel; spies offer only what they must.|
|**Circle of Light**|Strong allies — Circle wards and heals, Frostguard ensure they endure.|
|**Hollow Circle**|Distrust lingers — soldiers wary of corruption and secrecy.|
|**High Command**|Backbone of Resistance strategy; unwavering loyalty to Arlon and Hrovar.|

---

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs are tasked with holding a **strategic pass** against an overwhelming Oppressor assault until reinforcements arrive.
    
- A Frostveil scout patrol vanishes in the wilderness; the PCs must recover them before Oppressor forces strike.
    
- The party is promoted to **honorary field officers**, commanding Frostguard troops in a decisive battle.
    
- An Oppressor siege weapon threatens Frostgale; the PCs join the Stormbearers to dismantle it.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **disciplined, resilient, loyal.**
    
- The Frostguard represent **the Resistance’s military backbone** — reliable allies in open conflict.
    
- Useful as large-scale support in mass battles, or as quest-givers for escort/defence missions.
    
- Hrovar should embody stoic leadership, contrasting with more radical or covert wings.
