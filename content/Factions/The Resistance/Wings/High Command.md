## Overview

The **High Command** is the central leadership of the [[Resistance]], a coalition of trusted commanders who oversee operations across Vaeloris. Though each member governs their own division or sphere, it is here that the most critical decisions are debated and enacted.

The council is composed of strategists, mystics, logisticians, and regional leaders, each bringing their own perspective — and their own rivalries. The High Command embodies the Resistance’s unity, but also its tension: diverse voices bound by the single cause of overthrowing the Oppressors.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Direct all military, magical, and covert operations.
    
- Balance ideology, strategy, and resources across the wings.
    
- Unite diverse leaders into a single, functional war effort.
    
- Maintain morale and stability across Resistance territories.
    
- Deliver the decisive defeat of the Oppressors.
    

---

## Activities

- Holding war councils and strategic planning sessions.
    
- Allocating resources, supplies, and reinforcements to wings.
    
- Approving or vetoing major missions proposed by commanders.
    
- Managing relations with neutral regions and potential allies.
    
- Serving as the symbolic and practical leadership of the Resistance.
    

---

## Membership

- **[[Arlon Stormcrest]]** – Commander of the Resistance.
    
- **[[Sha’an Al-Duri]]** – Master of Logistics & Diplomacy.
    
- **[[Elder Mordwyn]]** – Sentinel of Ravenmoor (Hollow Circle).
    
- **[[Jarl Hrovar Ironhelm]]** – Warden of the North (Frostguard).
    
- **[[Kaelen Darkspear]]** – Emberblade Commander.
    
- **[[Nyra Whisperwind]]** – Spymaster of the Whisper Network.
    
- **[[Kara Thornveil]]** – Warden of the Circle of Light.
    

Each member represents both a wing of the Resistance and a worldview, shaping debates with their strengths and flaws.

---

## Ranks & Structure

- **Supreme Commander** – [[Arlon Stormcrest]], final authority.
    
- **Council Members** – Wing leaders and key strategists, equal in vote though unequal in influence.
    
- **Advisors** – Trusted figures invited for counsel (e.g. regional leaders, mystics).
    
- Decisions are typically majority vote, though Arlon holds veto power when consensus fails.

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs are invited to present evidence or arguments during a **High Command debate**, swaying the course of Resistance strategy.
    
- A council member goes missing or is sabotaged; the PCs must uncover who is behind it.
    
- PCs act as mediators when a **deadlock in High Command** threatens to split the wings.
    
- A vote on a dangerous operation hinges on the party’s past deeds or alliances.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **council of personalities**, where unity is fragile and every decision costs.
    
- High Command is the perfect setting for **political intrigue, faction drama, or moral dilemmas**.
    
- Each member should feel distinct in roleplay: Arlon (burdened leader), Kaelen (ruthless), Nyra (calculating), Kara (grounded), Mordwyn (cryptic), Sha’an (pragmatic), Hrovar (blunt).
    
- Use High Command meetings as narrative milestones — each decision marking a turning point in the war.
