_“We do not ward the world. We hold back what waits behind it.”_

---

## Overview

The **Hollow Circle** is the [[Resistance]]’s most secretive and feared wing — a hidden order of cursebreakers, ritualists, and occult guardians led by [[Elder Mordwyn]]. They confront the threats **no sword can slay**: abyssal corruption, cursed artefacts, and spectral incursions.

Their work is deliberately obscured from the wider Resistance. The less others know, the safer they remain.

---

## Goals & Focus

- Seal and contain cursed artefacts.
    
- Suppress abyssal entities and corrupted spirits.
    
- Monitor leyline corruption and arcane anomalies.
    
- Serve as High Command’s occult advisors.
    
- Banish or neutralise supernatural threats too dangerous for soldiers.
    

---

## Activities

- Performing counter-rituals to unravel infernal magic.
    
- Establishing and maintaining warded vaults for dangerous relics.
    
- Cleansing corrupted wounds, sites, or individuals.
    
- Investigating ruins, ancient texts, or anomalies linked to abyssal influence.
    
- Operating from the shadows, summoned only when containment fails.
    

---

## Membership

- Members are drawn from scholars, occultists, and warlocks who have turned against the abyss.
    
- **Symbol**: a hollow ring of thorns encircling a black spiral.
    
- **Initiation Rite**: _“The Silence Below”_ — surviving a solo vigil in the crypts against whispers from beyond.
    
- Common sayings include:
    
    - _“Better to be feared than followed.”_
        
    - _“We hold back what waits behind.”_
        
- Circle members are rarely remembered for their deeds — only for their failures.
    

---

## Ranks & Structure

- **Sentinel**: [[Elder Mordwyn]] – reserved, cryptic, profoundly learned.
    
- **Circle Keepers** – senior occultists overseeing sanctums and vaults.
    
- **Covenant Binders** – specialists in fiend contracts and sealing rituals.
    
- **The Unspoken** – masked operatives tasked with relocating or destroying artefacts.

## Adventure Hooks

- PCs must escort the Circle in retrieving and sealing a newly discovered cursed artefact.
    
- A warded crypt has been breached — the party must contain what has escaped.
    
- An initiation rite has gone wrong, unleashing madness into Gloomwatch’s vaults.
    
- Mordwyn asks the party to recover a forbidden tome before it falls into Oppressor hands.
    

---

## DM Notes

- Tone: **cryptic, unsettling, necessary evil.**
    
- The Hollow Circle are perfect for **occult mystery arcs**, cursed relic quests, or corruption-cleansing missions.
    
- Mordwyn should be played as **wise but haunted**, inspiring as much unease as trust.
    
- The Hollow Circle provides story hooks for horror, abyssal, or investigative playstyles.
