*Large aberration, Neutral Evil*  

---

## Overview  
Myrk is whispered of in hushed tones across the Frozen North — a **living shadow**, said to stalk his prey with the patience of a hunter and the precision of an assassin. Some call him Eryx’s blade in the dark, others a cautionary tale of a fallen knight consumed by the abyss. Further information indicates the host is Darin Blackstone, commander of the Paladins of the North 

Where once there was a man, now there is only a shifting mass of void and vengeance. Those who have survived his presence speak of a figure that **moves without sound**, **strikes without warning**, and **vanishes without a trace**.  

---

## Appearance  
Witnesses describe Myrk as a **nightmare given form**:  
- His body is a **shifting ink-black mass**, constantly writhing like living shadow.  
- **Sickly green eyes** pierce through the darkness, glowing like embers in the void.  
- His limbs elongate into **whip-like tendrils or blades**, capable of striking from the gloom.  
- Veins of **emerald abyssal energy** pulse across his body, flaring whenever he is about to attack.  

---

## Reputation & Rumours  
- Known as **“Eryx’s Assassin”**, he is said to eliminate targets of great importance to the Oppressors’ cause.  
- Soldiers claim he can **vanish into thin air**, reappearing behind his victims.  
- Tales warn that once Myrk has seen you, he can **track you no matter where you hide**.  
- Some whisper he was once a man — a knight who fell from grace — though the truth is lost in shadow.  

---

## Known Abilities (Rumoured)  
- **Silent Movement** – He blends seamlessly with the darkness.  
- **Shifting Form** – His arms become claws, blades, or tendrils at will.  
- **Predator’s Patience** – He stalks his prey for days, striking only when escape is impossible.  
- **Void Resilience** – Wounds that should fell a man seem to knit themselves closed unless seared by fire or holy light.  

---

## Common Knowledge  
Travellers, mercenaries, and Resistance members in the North agree on one thing:  
> *“If the shadows seem to move, it is already too late.”*  

---

## Threat Assessment  
- **Danger Level:** Extremely High.  
- **Recommended Response:** Evade at all costs. If confrontation is unavoidable, bring fire, radiant power, and overwhelming numbers.
